Consult (effort: high). One-shot, self-contained. Adversarial critique of a revised experiment design.

CONTEXT. Research project on AI debate with a limited "oracle verification" channel. A judge reads a two-sided debate about a question over a fictional world and picks an answer; in verification arms the judge can ask the oracle (an LLM with the full world text) yes/no/not-addressed questions. Banked findings so far:
- Phase 2 (82 questions, 3 hand-written worlds): sequential oracle protocol raised judge error +3.9pp [2.2, 5.7].
- Phase 3 (Qwen3.8 and Llama-3.3-70B judges, judge-selected adaptive queries, announced budgets 1/2/4/8 vs 0): error +3.8, +2.3, +2.4, +3.3pp, all Holm-significant; 8 vs 2 inconclusive (+0.97, p=.24). No monotone or U shape established.
- Mechanism audits on earlier data: roughly half of harmful flips were oracle-answer errors, half judge over-weighting of true-but-irrelevant confirmations (or reading NOT ADDRESSED as signal). Presenting the same Q&A as a neutral table roughly halved harm.
- Phase 4A: fixed captured histories crossed over between judges; the recipient matters (Qwen harmed, Llama slightly helped by the same histories).
- Phase 4B: an AI adjudication of oracle labels proposed corrections for 554 claims touching 45% of histories. The oracle in all phases was Llama-3.3-70B. Debaters were Llama-3.3-70B and Qwen3.7-Plus self-play.
- Question audit: only 5/82 old questions were source-determined; a new benchmark is needed.

PROPOSAL SENT TO PARTNER (Joey) on 9/20: 13 judges (GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra/Sol, GPT-6 Astra, Claude Haiku 4.5/Sonnet 5/Opus 5/Fable 5.1, DeepSeek V4 Flash/Pro, Qwen3.8 Flash and 2.4T, Llama 3.3 70B). Four conditions: world alone; debate only; debate + EIGHT FIXED pre-selected verification exchanges shared across judges; debate + whole world. 2,406 new generated scenarios (6 task types), both answer orders, target +/-2pp marginal CIs, ~$3.6-4.0k main judging within a $6k proposed ceiling (funding unreconciled). Debaters/oracle unspecified (text said "balancing Qwen and Llama donor sources").

JOEY'S CONCERNS:
1. Which models are oracle/debaters? They should be the most capable; judge-smarter-than-debater cases are uninteresting for scalable oversight; oracle should err as little as possible.
2. Why only 0/8/whole world? Wants 0/1/2/4/8, prefers more curve points over more models.
3. Scenario generation is unexplained.
4. Fixed pre-selected claims remove judge query selection by construction, eliminating a main proposed mechanism (judge picks irrelevant claims, over-updates) and are unrealistic.

MY REVISED DESIGN (my read: Joey is right on all four):
A. Debaters = frontier: Claude Fable 5.1 self-play on half the scenarios, GPT-6 Astra self-play on the other half, one transcript per scenario, generated once, judge-independent.
B. Judges restricted to models below the debaters: 5 judges (Luna, Sol, Haiku 4.5, Opus 5, Llama 3.3 70B for continuity). Astra/Fable dropped as judges (optionally a small ceiling anchor).
C. Oracle = frontier model with the full world (Opus 5 or Fable 5.1), chosen in the pilot by measured oracle accuracy against structured ground truth (never by judge outcomes). Oracle answers sampled and scored against the generator's fact table; oracle error rate reported.
D. Verification is adaptive: the judge writes its own queries. Budget curve 0/1/2/4/8 from ONE trajectory per judgment: the judge is NOT told its budget ("you may be stopped at any time"), queries one at a time, and at checkpoints 1/2/4/8 we fork a verdict request off a copy of the conversation (the main trajectory never sees the fork). Judge may stop early; actual query count recorded. Separate-arm alternative costs ~+$1.1k.
E. Selection diagnostic: debate + 8 queries chosen by a strong selector (Fable acting as judge), shown to every judge, vs each judge's own 8. Difference estimates the contribution of query selection vs interpretation.
F. Keep world-alone, debate-only, debate+whole-world.
G. Scenarios: procedurally generated structured fact base per world (entities, attributes, dated events, rules with exceptions, source attributions), rendered to prose by an LLM, back-extracted by a second model and diffed against the fact base; question and answer key computed from the fact base; wrong candidate = a specific reasoning slip (ignore exception, stale state, wrong scope); six task types; one question per independently generated world.
H. Precision: drop to +/-3pp (1,068 scenarios). My illustrative cost model (list batch rates, 4k-in/1k-out judge calls, 600 out per query turn, context +400/exchange, oracle with cached world): 5 judges +/-3pp total ~$3.0k (debates ~$0.5k, oracle ~$0.6k Opus, judging ~$1.9k); Fable oracle ~$3.6k; 5 judges +/-2pp ~$6.7k; 7 judges +/-3pp ~$3.9k.
