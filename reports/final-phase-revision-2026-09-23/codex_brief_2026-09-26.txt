Consult (effort: high). One-shot, self-contained. Adversarial check of a revised experiment design before replying to a research partner.

SETUP. AI-debate oversight study. A judge reads a two-sided debate (one honest, one dishonest debater) about a question over a short fictional world (~1,000-1,500 words) and picks one of two answers. In query arms the judge may ask a frontier oracle (which sees the full world) up to k yes/no questions, told its budget, may stop early (intention-to-treat). Debaters: Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra self-play (half the questions each), generated once, shared by all judges.

PHASE 3 DATA (82 questions, 3 hand-authored worlds, uncapped debates: median 411 words/turn, 2,465 words/debate; 12 judgments per judge per question per budget). Queries actually used: Llama 7.8 of 8, Qwen 7.2 of 8 (judges use nearly the full budget). Error rates by budget 0/1/2/4/8:
- Llama 3.3 70B: 11.5, 13.8, 11.4, 10.1, 10.1  (bump at 1, then below baseline)
- Qwen3.8: 6.1, 11.4, 10.7, 12.3, 13.9  (jump at 1, keeps rising)
Pooled vs b0: +3.8, +2.3, +2.4, +3.2pp (all significant). Question-level SD of the pooled (equal-weight two-judge) contrast: ~10pp for bk-b0, ~7.5pp for within-curve contrasts (b2-b1, b8-b2). Per-judge question-level SDs ~10-15pp. Pooling two judges cut SD by about sqrt(2), suggesting within-question noise dominates.

PARTNER'S LATEST POSITION:
(a) Wants a small debater canary before the full run (agreed).
(b) Expects the curve to saturate before 8 queries; suggests budgets 0/2/6 plus debate+whole world and world-alone (no debate). Hypothesis: the regression effect is real and peaks at a small number of queries (like Llama); wants the study able to show him right or wrong. Wants Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.6 Terra added so each frontier family has 3 tiers (Luna/Terra/Sol, Haiku 4.5/Sonnet 5/Opus 5), possibly cutting Llama.
(c) Rejects my structured fact-table world generator (worlds too simple, near-zero failure, "no room for debate"; real problems are ambiguous). Proposes: a frontier model authors more worlds/questions like the original three (interpretive questions, e.g. "Did the treaty produce a durable balance of power?", each with facts_required and a defensible wrong answer), and use world-alone (full world, no debate) to prune questions that are too ambiguous.
(d) Prefers cutting my Fable-chosen-query replay diagnostic to fund Sonnet/Terra. Alternative he floated: Fable ranks N queries by importance; judge gets Fable's top-k at the same k as its own; compare.

MY PLANNED REPLY / DESIGN:
1. Debater canary: ~40 debates (20 questions x 2 debaters), check side compliance, no refusals/meta-comments, length near Phase 3, plus a quick judge pass for difficulty. ~$20.
2. Budgets 0/1/2/6 (adding 1 because Llama's bump was at 1 and was gone by 2), plus debate+whole world and world-alone. 7 judges: Luna, Terra, Sol, Haiku 4.5, Sonnet 5, Opus 5, Llama (Llama is ~$290 of ~$3.3k total, and it is the model whose curve motivates the hypothesis). Pre-register his hypothesis as an intersection-union test: error at k (1 or 2, Holm over the two) exceeds error at both 0 and 6, pooled equal-weight across judges; per-judge shapes descriptive; 6 vs whole-world equivalence at a +/-1.5pp margin (TOST) for saturation.
3. Adopt his benchmark approach: Fable/Astra author ~85 new worlds x ~15 questions in the original format (keeping facts_required); prune with world-alone, but using a SEPARATE validator panel (Fable + Astra, both answer orders, cross-author) before debates are generated, with a frozen rule, so the judges' own world-alone arm stays a real measurement and the question set is the same for all judges. Target ~1,000 kept questions. Difficulty checked in the pilot against pre-set criteria, never against verification effects.
4. Cut the Fable replay arm. Use facts_required to auto-code whether each judge query touched a needed fact (descriptive mechanism evidence, no extra calls). Offer his top-k version at k=2 only as a ~$240 optional add-on.
5. Oracle: upgrade from Opus 5 to Fable or Astra (pick by pilot accuracy), since Opus and Sonnet are judges; +~$770.
Illustrative costs (list batch rates): 0/2/6 with 7 judges ~$2.9k; 0/1/2/6 ~$3.3k with Opus oracle, ~$4.1k with Fable oracle; + top-2 arm ~$4.3k. Prep ~$200. Proposed $6k ceiling, funding unreconciled.
Power (scaled from Phase 3 SDs to 14 judgments/question/condition, N~1,000): pooled MDE ~1.2-1.4pp for bk-b0, ~0.9-1.0pp within-curve; per judge ~2.5-3pp.
