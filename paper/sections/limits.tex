\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\joey{merge into the discussion as you see fit.}

Beyond the caveats stated with each result (Sections~\ref{sec:methods} and~\ref{sec:results}), three limits of the
design apply throughout; Appendix~\ref{app:limits} has the full list. All debates use one pairing of debaters. Each
debate was written once, under a warning that the judge might verify claims, and reused at every budget, so any
deterrent effect of that warning is in every condition, the no-query baseline included, and is not measured. The
benchmark, answer keys, oracle answers, audits and labels all come from models, with no human labels. And several models played more than one part: Claude
Fable 5.1 wrote half the worlds and was the dishonest debater, and Claude Opus 5.5 was a judge, the oracle, the gate
auditor and the labeller of judges' explanations, so the design cannot separate shared habits or errors among
related models from the effects it reports.
