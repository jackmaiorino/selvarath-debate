\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\joey{merge into the discussion as you see fit. Appendix~\ref{app:limits} has the full list.}

Beyond the caveats stated with each result (Sections~\ref{sec:analysis}, \ref{sec:peak}, \ref{sec:judges},
\ref{sec:gate} and~\ref{sec:why}), three limits of the design apply throughout. All debates use one pairing of
debaters, and each debate was written once and reused at every budget. The benchmark, answer keys, oracle answers,
audits and labels all come from models, with no human labels. And several models played more than one part: Claude
Fable 5.1 wrote half the worlds and was the dishonest debater, and Claude Opus 5.5 was a judge, the oracle, the gate
auditor and the labeller of judges' explanations, so the design cannot separate shared habits or errors among
related models from the effects it reports.
