   - **What would resolve it:** Clarify in Sections 3.4 and 4.4 that re-hosting triggered the audit, which revealed that `DeepSeek V4 Flash` itself admitted ~11% flagged queries (similar to the pilot gate), and distinguish *flagged share* from *verdict-affected share* on first mention; add one sentence in Appendix A (*"Uncertainty"*) and clearer captions in Figure 3 / Table 7 explaining why `bootstrap-t` produces `∞` on ultra-sparse per-judge cells while leaving pooled contrasts intact; and note in Appendix A (under Table 4) and parenthetically in Section 4.3 that `Holm p = 0.0545` includes the penalty for the two unrun `Llama` tests (`0.0327` across the four tests actually run).
