# Phase 4B: blinded oracle-label audit

4A qualified for this stage: the recipient contrast passed the statistical, practical and semantic gates. This audit locates defensible label corrections before testing whether they change recipient errors. The frozen [staged protocol](../rejudge/phase4_protocol_v1.json) remains the scientific authority.

**Fixed panel.** The 1,312 nonempty 4A histories contain 8,125 answered exchanges, deduplicated into 4,832 exact claims by world-document SHA256 plus exact claim text. Both adjudicators independently review every claim, for 9,664 requests. Their only supplied evidence is the world, exact claim and frozen label contract. They see no donor identity, original reply, candidate answers, verdicts, outcomes or each other's responses. Blocked exchanges are excluded from adjudication and preserved in the histories.

**Adjudicators.** Together endpoints `openai/gpt-oss-120b` and `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731` provide two families outside Qwen/Llama. Both use `temperature=1`, `top_p=1` and `max_tokens=8192`; reasoning effort is medium and low respectively. Their source-grounding accuracy is unmeasured. Exact endpoints, settings, prices, seeds, schedule and input hashes are recorded in the [execution proposal](../rejudge/phase4b_adjudication_2026-09-12.json) and private input manifest.

**Decision rule.** Retain only two agreeing, unambiguous labels with mechanically valid verbatim quotations. YES requires direct support; NO requires direct contradiction; NOT ADDRESSED requires identifying the unstated information and citing relevant context. Quotations cannot prove absence or semantic entailment. Unquotable absence, ambiguity, disagreement and malformed completed responses leave labels unchanged. Completed responses are never regenerated to improve formatting or agreement.

**Review and intervention.** At least 20 distinct claims across 10 questions and 40 packets must change. These are coverage checks, not power guarantees. A human source-checks up to 20 changed claims selected by the frozen hash ranking. Disagreement excludes that claim without replacement; pending review blocks recipient evaluation. After review, recheck coverage and freeze the edit map. There are 44,604 verified label spans across immediate replies and repeated recaps; all 1,704 blocked exchanges, assistant queries and other text remain fixed. The eventual 5,248 fresh recipient verdicts require their separately scoped cap. No recipient calls occur in this audit.

**Cost proposal.** Current uncached input/output rates per million are $0.15/$0.60 for GPT-OSS and $0.14/$0.28 for DeepSeek, confirmed against authenticated model metadata. Using a conservative input estimator, total cost scenarios are $14.94 at 512 completion tokens per call, $21.47 at 2,048, $30.18 at 4,096, and $47.59 at 8,192. Completion includes reasoning once. These exclude synthetic preflight and retries, and are scenarios rather than measured predictions. Proposed adjudication cap: **$100 total**, including a $1 preflight subcap and $10 uncertain-delivery subcap. The prior $250 approval was for 4A only. [GPT-OSS rates](https://www.together.ai/models/gpt-oss-120b), [DeepSeek rates](https://www.together.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash-0731).

**Operation.** Six synthetic endpoint checks precede the audit. Start four workers per model and measure increases up to 16 each. Durable SQLite reservations and responses permit resuming only missing work after a crash. During transport outages, probe an identical failed request with backoff within the cap. A ten-minute monitor is registered at launch and removed when collection finishes. No monitor or paid requests have been started by preparing this proposal.

**Offline validation.** All base artifacts, blinded messages and paired calls reproduced byte for byte. A full mock completed all 9,664 cells across a restart, retained and retried one unknown delivery, and repeated zero completed cells. Its deliberately invalid adjudications produced no released repair labels. This validation used no inference service and is not experimental evidence.
